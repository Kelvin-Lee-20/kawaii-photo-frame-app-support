💡 Frequently Asked Questions

How do I unlock all the premium frames?

You can unlock the entire collection of premium frames by making a one-time In-App Purchase. Just tap on any locked frame (the ones with the pink lock icon) and follow the on-screen instructions to upgrade!

I bought Premium, but my frames are still locked. How do I restore?

If you reinstall the app or get a new device, you don't need to pay again. Go to the Settings page and tap Restore Purchase. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

How do I move or zoom my photo inside the frame?

Once you select a frame, simply use your fingers to pinch (to zoom in/out) and drag your photo. You can also double-tap the photo to quickly reset it back to the center.

How do I save my finished photo to my phone?

Tap the pink Share button at the bottom right of the editing screen. From there, you can save the image directly to your Camera Roll, or share it instantly to Instagram, TikTok, Messages, and more!

The app feels slow or animations are lagging.

If you are using an older device, you can turn off the home screen animations to save battery and improve performance. Just go to the Settings page and toggle off Home Animations.
